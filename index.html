// About

BUILDING FOR
CREATORS

The African music industry generates billions in cultural value — but creators capture almost none of it. We are building the tools to change that.

NORTHHHERN LABS was founded by Benedict — a music producer, recording artist, and self-taught developer from Cross River State, Nigeria. He built the first version of KLAVE entirely from his phone while working without a laptop.

We build from first principles — every product starts from a problem we've lived ourselves. We ship fast, iterate ruthlessly, and build with intention. Our competitive advantage is that we are the market we're building for.

MUSIC FIRST
Every product we build starts from a deep understanding of the creative process. We are producers and artists before we are developers.
BUILT FOR AFRICA
We build products that work for African creators first — then scale globally. The infrastructure of the creative economy needs to work here too.
CULTURE FORWARD
We don't chase trends. We build at the edges of what's coming next — in sound, in technology, and in culture.
